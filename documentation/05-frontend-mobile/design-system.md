## 1. Brand Palette
- **Deep Green (`#1B5E3F`)**: Primary brand color, application headers, high-priority buttons.
- **Warm Gold (`#E0A526`)**: Secondary brand accent, highlights, badges, student status flags.
- **Dark Ink (`#1F2933`)**: Primary typography and high-contrast surfaces.
- **Off-White (`#FAF7F0`)**: Canvas and neutral background tone.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Card surfaces, elevated modals, data tables.
- **Border Gray (`#E5E7EB`)**: Structural dividers and input borders.

---

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Latin**: `Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- **Primary Ethiopic**: `Noto Sans Ethiopic, Abyssinica SIL, sans-serif`
