## 1. Design Philosophy
- **High Information Density**: Enable administrators and registrars to process large volumes of student records with minimal navigation friction.
- **Multilingual Support**: First-class support for English and Amharic / Ge'ez typography (`Noto Sans Ethiopic`).
- **Responsive Layouts**: Accessible across desktops, tablets, and mobile browsers.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Warm off-white surfaces (`#FAF7F0`) reduce eye fatigue; deep green (`#1B5E3F`) directs attention to primary actions.
